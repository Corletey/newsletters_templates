Welcome from the Secretariat

Dear Colleagues and Friends of the African Quality Assurance Network,

We are pleased to present this special conference edition of the African Quality Assurance Network newsletter, bringing you the key outcomes and reflections from our 2026 Annual Conference and General Assembly, held on July 21 and 22, 2026. Under the theme "Continental Collaboration for Quality Assurance in Africa," the gathering reaffirmed our collective commitment to strengthening quality assurance systems across the continent and deepening the partnerships that make this work possible.

This edition also covers the landmark Pan-African Quality Assurance Alliance Interim Council Meeting, which was convened alongside our General Assembly, as well as updates on the Harmonisation, Quality Assurance and Accreditation of African Higher Education Initiative and the African Higher Education and Research Space. We trust you will find these updates both informative and inspiring as we continue to advance quality in African higher education together.

2026 Annual Conference and General Assembly

The African Quality Assurance Network successfully hosted its 2026 Annual Conference on July 21 and 22, 2026, bringing together quality assurance professionals, higher education leaders, ministers responsible for education, and development partners from across Africa and beyond. The conference was held under the theme "Continental Collaboration for Quality Assurance in Africa," reflecting the network's enduring mission to foster cooperation among national quality assurance agencies and higher education institutions.

Over two days, participants engaged in plenary sessions, panel discussions, and workshops that addressed the most pressing challenges facing quality assurance in African higher education. Topics ranged from the implementation of the African Standards and Guidelines for Quality Assurance to the integration of digital technologies in institutional review processes. The General Assembly, held in conjunction with the conference, provided a vital forum for members to deliberate on governance matters, approve the annual report, and set strategic priorities for the year ahead.

"The strength of the African Quality Assurance Network lies in our ability to speak with one voice on matters of quality. This conference has demonstrated that when we collaborate across borders and institutions, we build systems that serve our students, our academics, and our continent with integrity and excellence."

- Message from the President, African Quality Assurance Network

Pan-African Quality Assurance Alliance

The African Union Commission, through the Department of Education, Science, Technology and Innovation, successfully concluded the Pan-African Quality Assurance Alliance Interim Council Meeting in July 2026. The meeting reaffirmed the continent's commitment to strengthening quality assurance, promoting academic mobility, and advancing the harmonisation of higher education across Africa.

His Excellency Professor Gaspard Banyankimbona, Commissioner for Education, Science, Technology and Innovation, welcomed participants and emphasised that the establishment of the Pan-African Quality Assurance Alliance represents a critical transition from the shared vision of the Pan-African Quality Assurance and Accreditation Framework toward a strong, recognised, and sustainable continental quality assurance structure.

 

Harmonisation Initiative

The Harmonisation, Quality Assurance and Accreditation of African Higher Education Initiative continues to serve as the backbone of continental quality assurance reform. Through its third phase, the initiative supports the development of an integrated and trusted African higher education space by promoting the continued use of continental quality assurance tools and fostering mutual recognition of qualifications.

Participants recognised that a strong and trusted continental quality assurance body will be essential to preserving the achievements of the Harmonisation, Quality Assurance and Accreditation of African Higher Education Initiative and supporting innovation, academic mobility, and regional integration across the continent.

African Union Decade of Education and Skills Development

The African Union Decade of Education and Skills Development runs from 2025 to 2034. Quality assurance is recognised as essential to the implementation of this decade, as well as to the Continental Education Strategy for Africa 2026 to 2035, the Science, Technology and Innovation Strategy for Africa, the Continental Technical and Vocational Education and Training Strategy, and Agenda 2063.

The African Quality Assurance Network remains fully committed to aligning its programmes with these continental frameworks, ensuring that every initiative we undertake contributes to the broader vision of an educated, skilled, and empowered Africa.

African Higher Education and Research Space

A dedicated panel at the conference examined the evolving concept and implementation paradigm of the African Higher Education and Research Space. Discussions focused on how quality assurance mechanisms must adapt to support cross-border academic collaboration, the mobility of students and researchers, and the mutual recognition of qualifications across African Union Member States.

Delegates emphasised that the African Higher Education and Research Space cannot be realised without robust, harmonised quality assurance systems that command the trust of governments, institutions, and employers alike. The African Quality Assurance Network was called upon to play a central coordinating role in developing these systems and in building the capacity of national agencies to operate within a continental framework.

 

"We must move from vision to action. The African Higher Education and Research Space is not a distant ambition; it is a practical necessity for the Africa we want. Quality assurance is the thread that binds this space together."
